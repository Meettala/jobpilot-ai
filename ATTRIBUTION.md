The artefact is generated by `tools/build_esco_vocabulary.py`, which traverses
the ISCO-08 classification through the public ESCO API. It contains occupation
labels, skill labels, alternative labels and the occupation-to-skill
relationships. It is a reformatting of ESCO content, not an independent work.

## Onward obligations

CC BY 4.0 requires attribution to travel with the data. If you reuse this
artefact, or build a product on it, you must keep the attribution above
visible to your users. It is not enough to leave it in this file and drop it
from the deployed application.

The artefact itself carries an `attribution` field for exactly this reason, so
the requirement survives being copied out of the repository.

## Scope note

ESCO is a European classification. It covers every field of work, but EU and
UK job titles and qualifications are its native vocabulary. US-specific
credentials map less cleanly. An official ESCO–O\*NET occupation crosswalk
exists if wider coverage becomes necessary.

## Artefact provenance

Generated 2 August 2026 by `tools/build_esco_vocabulary.py` via full ISCO-08
traversal of the public ESCO API. Contents: 2,909 occupations, 12,549 skills,
59,509 essential-skill links, 78,713 skill alternative labels and 29,722
occupation alternative labels, spanning all ten ISCO major groups.

Five skill concepts could not be enriched with alternative labels after
retries; they are flagged `enrichmentFailed` in the artefact and retain the
preferred label captured from their occupation link.
